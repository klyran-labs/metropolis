# ComputeX Acceptable Use Policy & Compliance Framework

## 1. Scope & Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs access to decentralized GPU and compute resources coordinated via ComputeX. All compute buyers, quantitative market makers, and node operators must comply with this policy.

---

## 2. Prohibited Workloads
Compute instances provisioned through ComputeX may NOT be utilized for:
1. Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, botnet command-and-control, or malicious port scanning.
2. Unconsented cryptocurrency mining or proof-of-work exploitation outside of expressly listed compute pools.
3. Distribution of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), malware, ransomware, or illicit credential harvesting.
4. Exploitation of unauthorized network proxies or illegal bypass of trade sanctions.

---

## 3. Compliance & Abuse Sentinel
- Node operators and automated telemetry probes monitor network egress for abnormal bandwidth spikes matching DDoS patterns.
- Verified abuse complaints result in immediate lease revocation without refund and reporting to relevant jurisdictional authorities.

---
